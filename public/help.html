How to use EKA Builder

Describe a business, pick the designs you like, and get a fast, finished website you can change by asking the AI or by hand.

Getting started

On Windows

  1. Install Node.js LTS from nodejs.org (Next, Next, Finish).
  2. Download the builder as a ZIP, right-click it, choose Extract All, and open the extracted folder. Don't run it from inside the ZIP.
  3. Double-click start-windows.bat. The first start installs what it needs, then opens the builder in your browser.
  4. The first time, a random password is created and shown in the window. It's also saved in settings.txt.

To update later, download the new ZIP and double-click update-windows.bat in your builder folder. Your websites and settings are kept.

Logging in

Go to /admin on the builder's address and enter the password from settings.txt. Forgotten it? Open settings.txt in Notepad, change ADMIN_PASSWORD, save, and restart the builder.

Making a new website

Click + New website. There are four short steps:

  1. Brief: the business name and what they do, plus optional logo, photos of the business, phone, email, address, opening hours, social links, brand colours, a style and which pages they need.
  2. Pages: a sentence or two about what goes on each page. Leave one blank and it's written from the business description.
  3. Homepage: you're shown a homepage design. Click Generate another for a different layout and flick between them. Type changes in the boxes for the whole site or any page.
  4. Other pages: each page gets the same treatment: Use this design, Generate another, or describe a change. When every page is picked, the site is built. Build the site now skips ahead; unpicked pages are designed automatically.
Photos are filled with tasteful placeholder pictures in the site's colours. In the editor they're marked “Placeholder”: double-click one to swap in a real photo.

Without an AI key, designs come from built-in templates using everything in the brief, and the changes you type are saved as a to-do list in the editor's AI tab.

The editor

The page is in the middle. On the left are five tabs: Add (blocks and ready-made sections), AI, Pages, Style (the whole site's look) and Site (address, hosting, forms). On the right are the settings for whatever you've selected.

  • Select anything by clicking it. Sections are the full-width bands; blocks are the pieces inside them.
  • Add by dragging from the Add tab onto the page, or select something and click a block to add it after.
  • Move by dragging the ⠿ handle on the selection bar, or with the ↑ ↓ buttons.
  • Edit text by double-clicking it on the page. Bold, italic and links work as you'd expect.
  • See it on other screens with the 🖥 ▭ 📱 buttons at the top.
  • Layers (left) lists everything on the page: click to select, ↑ ↓ to move, ◉ to hide.
  • Everything saves automatically. The ▾ next to undo lists recent changes so you can go back several at once; History keeps saved versions.

Styling

The whole site: the Style tab on the left

Colour presets and colours, fonts, text sizes, corner rounding, content width, space between sections, buttons (size, shape, weight, capitals, shadow and hover animation), and an animation for sections as they scroll into view.

One item: the panel on the right

Select a block or section and use its three tabs:

  • Content (or Layout for sections): what it says and shows, columns, background choice.
  • Style: font, size, weight, capitals, italic, line height, letter spacing, text colour; background colour or gradient, background image or video, tint; border, corner radius and shadow; for buttons, size and hover animation.
  • Advanced: margin and padding (separately for desktop, tablet and phone), max width, min height, entrance animation with speed and delay, hover effect, and hiding on desktop, tablet or phone.
For phone- or tablet-only spacing, click 📱 or ▭ next to Margin or Padding (or at the top of the editor) and type the values. Empty boxes use the bigger screen's value.

Eye-catching effects

  • Text effects (a heading's or text's Style tab): words that blur or slide in one by one, a shine that sweeps across, a moving colour gradient, or a highlighter underline that draws itself.
  • Scrolling strip block: logos, reviews or selling points gliding across the page.
  • Count-up numbers: tick the option in a Stats block.
  • 3D tilt hover effect, and Slowly move the gradient for gradient backgrounds.

They're all done with CSS alone, so they don't slow the site down. Use them sparingly: one effect on the hero heading looks polished; one on every heading looks busy.

Animations play on the live site and in Preview, not while you're editing. Use ▶ Preview animation to see one. Visitors who've turned off motion on their device don't see them.

Header, footer & menu

Click the header or footer on the page, or use Edit header / Edit footer on the right when nothing is selected. Changes apply to every page.

  • Content: logo text or image, logo size (and on phones), the header button and its style, sticky header, menu links, footer text and links.
  • Style: background, text colour, font, border, shadow, padding, and the Menu links group: font, size, weight, capitals, spacing, colours (normal, hover, current page), hover effect (colour, underline, line above, pill) and position (right, centre, left).

Call / WhatsApp button: in Site → Business details, choose a contact button and it stays in the corner of the screen on phones, so visitors can ring or message the business in one tap.

The menu is built from your pages automatically. Untick that option in the header to write your own links. Want the logo somewhere else too? Add a Logo block.

Images, galleries & video

Uploaded photos are resized and converted to WebP automatically, with a smaller copy for phones, so big phone photos don't slow the site down. Images load only when they're about to be seen.

Stock photos

With a free stock photo key, new designs get real photos that suit the business instead of placeholder artwork, and the image picker gets a stock photo search. The photos are free to use on websites.

  1. Make a free account at pixabay.com.
  2. While logged in, go to pixabay.com/api/docs. Your key is shown on that page (in the "Parameters" table, next to key).
  3. Paste it after PIXABAY_API_KEY= in settings.txt, save, and restart the builder.

A Pexels key (PEXELS_API_KEY=) works too, if you have one; Pexels has paused giving out new keys.

Galleries

Choose columns for desktop, tablets and phones, how many rows to show, the gap, the photo shape, grid or masonry layout, a hover effect, captions, and whether clicking a photo opens it full size (with next/previous arrows).

Background images

In a section's or block's Style tab: choose the image, a different one for phones, which part to keep in view, fill or fit, stay still while scrolling, or repeat as a pattern. Add a tint (a colour or gradient at any strength) to make text readable, an effect (fade in, slow zoom, zoom out, slow pan, parallax) and blur, black & white or brightness.

In new designs these are set for you: the AI picks which sections get a photo background and sets the tint, focus point and effect. It uses the business's own photos first (add them in the wizard's first step, with a note on what each shows), then a matching stock photo. The templates used without AI add a tint and slow zoom to their photo backgrounds too.

Background video

Upload an MP4 (best: 10–20 seconds, 1280px wide, a few MB) or paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. It plays silently on a loop. Phones show the background image instead unless you tick Play on phones too. Builder previews show the image, because previews block autoplay; the video plays on the live site.

Pages & Google

The Pages tab adds, renames, reorders and deletes pages. With nothing selected, the right-hand panel shows the page's name, web address, and the title and description Google shows in search results (aim for 120–155 characters).

Using AI

The AI designs new sites and makes changes you describe, for example “add a pricing section”, “make it feel more luxurious” or, with something selected, “make this punchier”. Every AI change can be undone.

Turning it on

  1. Create an account at console.anthropic.com, add a payment method, and create an API key.
  2. Open settings.txt, paste the key after ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=, save, and restart the builder.

You pay Anthropic for what you use. Designs and new sites use the strongest model so the first designs need few changes; changes use a faster, cheaper one and send back only what changed. Instructions and the approved design are cached between calls, so repeat reads cost a fraction of the price. Without a key everything else still works: designs come from templates and your requests become a to-do list in the AI tab.

What it costs: the AI this month button on the dashboard shows the estimated spend this month, by type of job and by website. Your Anthropic bill is the exact figure.

Contact forms

Add a Contact form block and choose its fields. On sites hosted by the builder, messages appear under Site → Messages received here. On a customer's own hosting, they're emailed to the address in Site → Contact forms. Forms include spam protection.

Customer review & the site check

Getting the customer's feedback

In Site → Customer review, click Create a review link and send it to the customer. They see your latest draft (not the live site) and, without logging in, can press 💬 Comment, click any part of the page and say what they'd like changed, then press Approve when they're happy. Their comments appear in the same place in the editor: Show jumps to the part they mean, and ✓ Done ticks it off. You can turn the link off at any time.

The site check

When you press Publish, the builder checks for things visitors would notice: placeholder pictures, leftover template text, buttons that link nowhere or to missing pages, text that's hard to read, and a contact form with nowhere to send messages. It also suggests improvements like image descriptions and Google descriptions. Show takes you to each one. You can run it any time from the top of the Site tab.

Publishing

  • Preview shows your latest changes privately. Publish makes them live.
  • On the builder's own server, each site gets an address, and you can add the customer's domain in Site → Address.
  • On the customer's own hosting: in Site → Your hosting (FTP), enter the FTP details from their hosting control panel, click Test connection, then Save. From then on, Publish uploads the site there. Only changed files are sent.
  • Prefer to upload by hand? Download the published site as files gives you a ZIP for any web host.

Published pages are plain HTML with no JavaScript, so they load very quickly.

Online shops

For a shop, install CubeCart (free) on the hosting to run the basket, payments and orders, and design the look in EKA Builder. In Site → Online shop (CubeCart), click Download as CubeCart skin, upload the ZIP into CubeCart's skins folder, extract it, and choose it in the CubeCart admin. Instructions are in the ZIP.

Demo mode

With DEMO_MODE=1 in the settings, the login page shows Try the demo. Each visitor gets a private workspace without a password. They can do everything except publish or upload to hosting, AI use is capped, and their sites are deleted after a day.

Settings

Settings live in settings.txt (Windows) or environment variables (servers). Restart the builder after changing them.

SettingWhat it does
ADMIN_PASSWORDYour login password.
ANTHROPIC_API_KEYTurns on AI.
AI_MODEL, AI_EDIT_MODELThe AI models for new sites and for changes.
BASE_DOMAINYour domain for site addresses, e.g. sites.example.com gives name.sites.example.com.
PORT, HOSTWhere the builder listens (default port 3000).
DATA_DIRWhere websites are stored (default: the data folder).
SUPPORT_EMAILShown on the Support page.
DEMO_MODE1 turns on the public demo. Also DEMO_HOURS (default 24), DEMO_SITES (3), DEMO_AI_PER_VISITOR (15) and DEMO_AI_PER_DAY (300).

Troubleshooting

“The password is wrong”

Check ADMIN_PASSWORD in settings.txt (no spaces around it), save, and restart the builder.

The builder window closes straight away on Windows

Make sure you extracted the ZIP first and that Node.js LTS is installed. Run start-windows.bat again and read the message in the window.

“Port in use”

The builder tries the next free port and shows the address it used. Or set PORT to another number.

FTP won't connect

Double-check the host, username and password in the hosting control panel. Some hosts need the folder set to public_html. Use Test connection to see the exact error.
